CaterDesk Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · applies to the CaterDesk Android app (com.flavorfusion.expenses) and the web app at expense-calculator-kohl.vercel.app

In short

CaterDesk keeps your business records on your own phone. There is no account and no sign-in. CaterDesk has no ads or analytics, and nobody tracks you. The developer never receives your events, expenses, vendors, clients, payments, menus or bill photos.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your device. That covers events, guest counts, menus, quotes, expenses, vendors and their phone numbers, client and vendor payments, bill photos and your business details. It is not uploaded to any server. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage or lose the phone, these records are gone unless you made a backup.

  • Photos of bills come from the camera or gallery only when you choose to add one, and are stored on the phone.
  • Contacts: when you tap the contacts button, Android lets you pick one contact. CaterDesk receives only that contact's name and phone number and saves them as a vendor. It cannot read your other contacts.
  • Privacy PIN: it hides the screen. It is stored on the phone as a one-way hash.

Backups

Backups are made only when you tap a backup button. An encrypted backup is locked with a recovery key that is made on your phone and never leaves it. The developer cannot open your encrypted backups. When you choose Backup to Google Drive, the file goes to the app you pick in Android's share menu, under your own account. CaterDesk does not sign in to Google and has no access to your Drive. A readable backup is not encrypted: anyone with the file can read it, so keep it private.

What we do not do

  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No location, microphone or background access.
  • The app is meant for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Error reports can be turned off at any time in Settings.
  • Your records are deleted when you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. Nothing else holds a copy, apart from backups you made yourself.
  • Error reports cannot be linked to you, so there is nothing personal to request or delete on the server. They are kept in the hosting logs for a limited time.
