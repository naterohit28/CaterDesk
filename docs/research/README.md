## Notes for Claude

Evaluate the evidence and conclusions before proposing naming or product changes. The report is a dated research snapshot, not an implementation specification or trademark clearance. Its description of our app reflects the build discussed when the research was prepared; inspect the current source and release before treating those feature comparisons as current.

Check:

1. Whether each cited source supports its claim, distinguishing app titles, publisher names, company names and websites.
2. Whether uncertain or unavailable sources need rechecking. In particular, direct retrieval of caterdesk.co.uk timed out, and a secondary trademark lead was not verified against an official registry.
3. The closest competitors, especially CateringWaala, against the current app's expense, vendor-credit, payments, profitability, language and offline workflows. Vendor feature descriptions were not verified through hands-on testing.
4. Whether to retain the name for personal use or screen a different public brand before commercial expansion. No domain availability or legal permission is established by this report.
5. A prioritized recommendation with confirmed findings, unresolved questions and proposed next steps clearly separated.

Apple evidence covers six public Search API queries across India, the US and UK. No exact returned title match does not prove worldwide absence. Google Play exact-title absence was not established exhaustively.

This handoff authorizes evaluation, not an automatic rename, data migration, domain purchase, customer contact or feature implementation. Preserve business records and existing app identities while assessing options.
