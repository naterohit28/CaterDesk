# CaterDesk: name conflicts and competing apps

Research date: **3 November 2026**. Prepared for the owner of the custom Expense-Calculator/CaterDesk app.

## Decision

**Similar apps exist, and CaterDesk is already used in catering technology. I would choose a different public-facing name before buying a domain for a commercial launch.** This is a branding recommendation based on observed overlap, not a finding that your use infringes a trademark.

My earlier suggestion to reserve caterdesk.in was premature because it did not include a name-conflict search. Domain availability has not been established in this research. A missing exact app-store title would not undo the confirmed web and publisher-name conflicts.

The app's current features remain useful for its owner. Competition does not mean that building a focused personal tool was wasted work. It does mean that expense tracking, vendor balances and event profitability should not be presented as an invention with no competitors.

## 2. App-store name checks

### Apple App Store

I queried Apple's public Search API for **caterdesk** and **cater desk**, separately in **India, the United States and the United Kingdom**, using software results with a limit of 50. None of the returned titles was an exact CaterDesk/Cater Desk match.

| Storefront | caterdesk: returned results | cater desk: returned results | Exact title found |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| India | 0 | 3 | No |
| United States | 36 | 40 | No |
| United Kingdom | 41 | 42 | No |

Some queries returned fuzzy matches such as Autodesk and unrelated products. These counts are search responses, not the number of competing apps or an exhaustive count of the store. The public API can omit products, and titles can vary by storefront.

Reproducible endpoints and exact-match results are saved in `CaterDesk-Name-Search-Evidence-2026-11-03.json`. Example: [Apple API query, India](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=caterdesk&country=in&entity=software&limit=50).

[Feedr: Cloud Canteen on Apple's UK store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/feedr-cloud-canteen/id1478626418) is titled Feedr and names Feedr as developer. Do not misreport it as an iPhone app called CaterDesk merely because the Android publisher uses that name.

### Google Play

Exact-name and spaced-name web searches were run against Google Play app listings, followed by direct listing checks. I did not verify an app whose displayed title was exactly CaterDesk. I did verify the Caterdesk publisher on Feedr.

The India Play search-results page could not be retrieved through the research browser. Therefore the conclusion is **no exact-title listing verified**, not **no such app exists anywhere on Play**. Regional, unlisted, removed, private and newly published apps may not appear in indexed results.

### Additional web-based competitors

Food-ordering marketplaces and general wedding-planning apps were separated from direct caterer-business software. Feedr is useful evidence of the name conflict, but its employee meal-ordering workflow is not a close replacement for your expense ledger.

## 4. Why CateringWaala deserves the first comparison

Its store listings cover vendor hisaab with payables and payments, categorized spending and booking-wise profit. They also describe Marathi support, making the similarity more specific than generic expense tracking. [Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cateringwaala.app), [iOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/cateringwaala-ai-assistant/id6760256578).

Its website advertises a free core plan and an AI Standard plan at ₹599/month. Treat that as a dated vendor offer, not a verified lifetime promise or assurance that every feature is free. Confirm actual signup terms, taxes, usage limits, export availability and cancellation terms before relying on the pricing. [Vendor website](https://cateringwaala.com/).

For a useful hands-on comparison, create synthetic data: one event, one vendor credit purchase, two partial payments, a bill image and a client advance. Check final balances, event profit, duplicate-save handling, edit behavior, backup/restore and what happens without internet. Do not upload your real customer data simply to test a competitor.

## 5. What remains distinctive about your app

Our current build is a focused, offline-first tool with local IndexedDB records, event expenses, vendor-credit balances, payments, receipt attachments, English/Marathi UI, manual backup/restore and category/subcategory vendor suggestions. It is owner-controlled and can be adapted to your own entry habits. These statements come from the app we built and tested, not from competitor marketing.

The research does not prove this feature combination is unique. In particular, Marathi, vendor ledgers and event profit are already advertised together elsewhere. The stronger potential positioning is **fast, reliable, owner-controlled event accounts for small caterers**, with demonstrable advantages in entry speed, reconciliation and recovery. That positioning is a hypothesis to test with users.

For a commercial version, the important gaps in our current product include automatic backup/recovery, optional multi-device/team access, permissions and audit history, quote/invoice workflows and tested onboarding. Existing competitors make these useful comparison areas. They are not a request to implement all of them now.

## 6. Naming recommendation and limits

I would pause spending on public CaterDesk branding and domain promotion, select several more distinctive candidate names, and screen each across web search, both app stores, relevant company records, domains and trademark registries before choosing one.

This research is not an India/UK/EU/worldwide trademark clearance. A secondary company-directory result reported a historical CATERDESK word-mark entry, but I did not verify its current official registration, classes, owner or territory, so it is not treated as a confirmed trademark finding. [Secondary lead](https://www.companyhouse.de/Feedr-GmbH-Berlin).

No app installation, paid subscription, private account access, domain purchase or contact with other businesses was performed. No code, app identity, data or repository settings were changed for this research. Your present installation does not need an emergency data migration just because the public-facing name deserves reconsideration.

## 7. Method and coverage limits

- Sources: broad and exact-name web searches; Google Play indexed/direct listings; Apple's public App Store search API in IN/US/GB; product websites; UK Companies House; historical trade coverage.
- Search variants included CaterDesk, Caterdesk and Cater Desk, plus catering management, expenses, vendors, profit, menu costing and Indian catering workflows.
- Primary product/store pages were preferred for feature claims. Historical press and the unverified trademark lead are explicitly labeled.
- Search results and vendor pages can be stale. Some pages timed out; Apple search results can differ from browser descriptions. No unsupported download, revenue, customer-count or quality ranking is used to establish market leadership.
- Not covered exhaustively: every national storefront, every unlisted/private APK, closed enterprise products, domain ownership history, paid trademark databases, source-code similarity and security audits of competitors.

**Practical conclusion:** useful comparable apps exist; the most relevant first trial is CateringWaala; and CaterDesk is already a crowded name within the same industry. Keep the current tool working, but pick a better-cleared public brand before commercial expansion.
